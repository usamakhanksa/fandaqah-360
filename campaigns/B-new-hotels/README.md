# Campaign B · New independent properties

**Goal:** qualified walkthroughs with independent hotels and eligible accommodation properties in Saudi Arabia, leading to paid subscriptions.
**Landing route:** shared URL with `utm_campaign=B_new_hotels_2610…` → `/new-hotels/` (see `links.csv`).
**Call to action:** Explore a simpler way to run my hotel · اكتشف طريقة أبسط لإدارة فندقي

## The one question every asset answers
"Why should I change from my current setup?" The answer is always the owner's day, not the software: simpler operations,
less manual work, a clearer view of the property, central management and fewer vendors to deal with. The final impression
is **"This is an easier way to run my property"**, never "this software has many modules".

| Beat | English | Arabic |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge | Still running your hotel on separate systems? | ما زلت تدير فندقك بأنظمة متفرقة؟ |
| Outcome | Run more of your hotel from one platform, with fewer vendors to manage. | أدِر جزءاً أكبر من فندقك من منصة واحدة، مع موردين أقل. |
| Proof | Enter it once. See it everywhere. | أدخِل البيانات مرة واحدة، وشاهدها في كل مكان. |
| Action | Explore a simpler way to run your hotel. | اكتشف طريقة أبسط لإدارة فندقك. |

Module names appear only on the landing page's catalog, under benefit headings, never as the message.

## Buyer
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Independent property owner or GM; finance and operations influence the choice |
| Problem | Separate tools and vendors, the same data typed twice, month-end built by hand, unclear responsibility between suppliers |
| Objections | Migration effort, training, the cost of switching |
| What answers it | The page's rollout section (migration assessment, training plan, support scope) and terms confirmed before signing |

## Offer
Launch price: SAR 3,495 a year (excl. VAT), on a one-year subscription, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Standard pricing
applies after the launch. There is **no two-year subscription or commitment**: never write "two-year subscription", "fixed for
two years" or "lock the price for two years". Whether new hotels can also secure an additional year at the same rate (as
existing customers can) is not confirmed for B, so do not mention it.

## Audience and exclusions
- New independent hotels and eligible accommodation properties in Saudi Arabia.
- A Ministry registration record alone does not establish the buyer, current PMS or permission to send promotions.
- Exclude existing customers (A) and accounts assigned to C or D.

## Channels
1. **Google Search**, high intent (see `search-ads.csv`, keywords below). Benefit headlines lead; PMS keywords stay in rotation.
2. **Short Arabic social videos** (Meta Reels; other platforms only if approved). Use the reel and carousel.
3. **Direct follow-up** only to verified, permitted contact records (`outreach.md`; LinkedIn sequence in the [LinkedIn kit](https://usamakhanksa.github.io/fandaqah-360/linkdein-message.html)).

## Sales handoff
Qualify: decision-maker, systems and vendors in use, where work is typed twice, property type and size, timeline. Book a
walkthrough **built around how the property runs today** (the form's "Selected interest" and company details arrive in the
CRM). Publish package limits and usage charges only after they are confirmed.

## Scorecard
Qualified property enquiries · attended walkthroughs · proposals · paid subscriptions · collected cash.
Do not promise zero downtime, instant migration or regulatory approval without evidence.
