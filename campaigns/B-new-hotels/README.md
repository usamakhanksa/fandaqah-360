# Campaign B · New independent properties

**Goal:** qualified demos with independent hotels and eligible accommodation properties in Saudi Arabia, leading to paid subscriptions.
**Landing route:** shared URL with `utm_campaign=B_new_hotels_2610…` → `/new-hotels/` (see `links.csv`).
**Call to action:** Book my hotel demo · احجز عرضاً توضيحياً لفندقي

## Buyer
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Independent property owner or GM; finance and operations influence the choice |
| Problem | Separate tools, repeated entry, unclear responsibility between suppliers |
| Objections | Migration effort, training, committing for two years |
| What answers it | The page's rollout section (migration assessment, training plan, support scope) and terms confirmed before signing |

## Audience and exclusions
- New independent hotels and eligible accommodation properties in Saudi Arabia.
- A Ministry registration record alone does not establish the buyer, current PMS or permission to send promotions.
- Exclude existing customers (A) and accounts assigned to C or D.

## Channels
1. **Google Search**, high intent (see `search-ads.csv`, keywords below).
2. **Short Arabic social videos** (Meta Reels; other platforms only if approved). Use the reel and carousel.
3. **Direct follow-up** only to verified, permitted contact records (`outreach.md`).

## Sales handoff
Qualify: decision-maker, current systems, pain, property type and size, timeline. Book a demo **tailored to the property**
(the form's "Selected interest" and company details arrive in the CRM). Publish package limits and usage charges only after
they are confirmed.

## Scorecard
Qualified property enquiries · attended demos · proposals · paid subscriptions · collected cash.
Do not promise zero downtime, instant migration or regulatory approval without evidence.
