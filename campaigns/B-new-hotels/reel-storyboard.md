Arabic master with burned-in captions; English cut where needed. Real interface with **sample data**. Safe area: 250 px top and bottom.
Benefit first: the reel answers "why change from my current setup?" and ends on one impression, an easier way to run the property.
Keyframes: `creatives/reel-frames/B-reel-1…4-ar.jpg` (and `-en`). Rendered reels: `book/media/B-new-hotels/reels/`.

**English voice-over (20–25 s):** "See how much simpler your hotel can run, at the launch price."

product demo, and the offer follows the corrected terms: a one-year subscription at the launch price, never a two-year commitment.
