# Campaign B · Outreach pack

Only for verified contacts with documented permission for the channel. A public directory or Ministry record is **not**
marketing consent; use those lists for research and Search/contextual targeting only. The LinkedIn sequence lives in the
[LinkedIn kit](https://usamakhanksa.github.io/fandaqah-360/linkdein-message.html).

## Qualification questions (record in the CRM)
1. Property type, rooms or units, city.
2. Systems and vendors in use today (PMS, accounting, POS) and what is painful.
3. Where work is typed twice or reports are built by hand.
4. Who decides, who influences (finance, operations).
5. Timeline and what would make them switch.

## Follow-up rules
Respond within the agreed target during staffed hours · at most three follow-ups across seven business days · log every attempt.
