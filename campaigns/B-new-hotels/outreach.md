## Qualification questions (record in the CRM)
1. Property type, rooms or units, city.
2. Systems in use today (PMS, accounting, POS) and what is painful.
3. Who decides, who influences (finance, operations).
4. Timeline and what would make them switch.

## Follow-up rules
Respond within the agreed target during staffed hours · at most three follow-ups across seven business days · log every attempt.
