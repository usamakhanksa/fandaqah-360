# Campaign A · Outreach pack

Send only to verified existing customers with recorded permission for the channel. Respect opt-outs immediately.
Use the tagged links from `links.csv` (never add personal details to a link).

## Account manager (call or message)
> مرحباً {{الاسم}}، معك {{اسم مدير الحساب}} من فندقة. نراجع حالياً اشتراكات عملائنا لنرى أين يمكن لفندقة 360 أن تضيف قيمة حقيقية.
> هل يناسبك أن نخصّص 20 دقيقة لمراجعة اشتراك {{اسم الفندق}} الحالي والوحدات التي قد تفيد فريقك، وأهليتك لعرضنا المدفوع مسبقاً لمدة سنتين؟

*English:* Hello {{name}}, this is {{account manager}} from Fandaqah. We are reviewing customer subscriptions to see where
Fandaqah 360 can add real value. Could we take 20 minutes to review {{hotel}}'s current plan, the modules your team could use
next and your eligibility for our two-year prepaid offer?

## Follow-up rules (playbook p.8)
- Confirm receipt and assign a sales owner. Proposed target: respond within one business hour during staffed hours.
- At most **three** follow-ups across **seven business days** for a valid enquiry with no reply, unless the person opts out or asks for another time.
- Log every attempt and response in the CRM.
