# Campaign A · Reel storyboard (25 s, 1080×1920)

Make the problem visible in the first three seconds. Show the **live product with sample data**; no guest identities.
Captions burned in (most views are muted). Keep text inside the safe area: 250 px clear at the top and bottom.
Keyframes for the editor: `creatives/reel-frames/A-reel-1…4-ar.jpg` (and `-en`).

**The thread:** the three manual records in the hook (purchase spreadsheet, paper room log, month-end by hand) are the
modules ticked at 10–20 s: purchasing, housekeeping, accounting. Set them up in the first shot so the proof pays them off.
