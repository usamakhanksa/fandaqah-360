# Campaign C · Sales brief

Three documents per account: an **operations overview** for the GM or owner, a **technical brief** for IT and a **cost
comparison** for finance. Same story, different proof. Lead with the outcome; the integration detail follows.

## 1. Before the first call (qualification)
| Confirm | Why |
|---|---|
| Systems in use today for HR, procurement, inventory, finance and reporting | Shows what can be consolidated |
| Where work is repeated or visibility is missing | The outcome the review has to deliver |
| Installed PMS product and **version** | Compatibility depends on the exact release |
| Deployment (cloud / on-premise / brand-hosted) | Changes interface access and setup |
| Interface access and who grants it | Brand, owner or vendor approval |
| Brand or owner approval route | Who can say yes, and how long it takes |
| Decision-makers: GM/owner, IT, finance | One conversation with all three |

Until these are confirmed, **do not promise compatibility**, timelines or prices.

## 2. Operations overview for the GM or owner (one page)
- **The challenge:** HR, procurement, inventory, finance, compliance and reporting often run on separate systems. That means
  duplicated work, higher costs and limited visibility across departments.
- **What changes:** Fandaqah brings those operations into one platform. Opera stays your core PMS, and your brand standards
  do not change.
- **What you get:** fewer systems, a clearer view of the hotel, and room to add outlets or properties on the same platform.
- **The first objective:** find where processes can be simplified, consolidated or improved, without disrupting the PMS.

## 3. Technical brief for IT (one page)
- **What stays:** your existing PMS remains the system of record for its scope.
- **What Fandaqah adds:** HR, procurement and inventory, finance and reporting, and compliance workflows (ZATCA, Shomoos,
  NTMP), connected through approved interfaces only.
- **What we need from you:** product/version, deployment, interface documentation or access route, required workflows.
- **How we confirm:** technical meeting, integration assessment, written scope in the proposal.
- **What we never claim:** certification, partnership or endorsement by the PMS vendor.

## 5. Meeting agenda (45 minutes)
1. How the hotel runs today: systems, repeated work, reporting gaps (10)
2. The outcomes that matter: what to simplify, consolidate or improve (10)
3. How Fandaqah works around the PMS: what stays, what it adds, how it connects (15)
4. Next steps: compatibility check and scoped proposal (10)

## 6. Red lines
No "50% savings" · no claim that Fandaqah replaces or changes the PMS · no unsupported integration · no unapproved price ·
no unlimited customisation · no guaranteed savings · no third-party logos.
