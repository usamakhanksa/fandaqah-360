# Campaign C · Sales brief

Two documents per account: a **technical brief** for IT and a **cost comparison** for finance. Same story, different proof.

## 1. Before the first call (qualification)
| Confirm | Why |
|---|---|
| Installed PMS product and **version** | Compatibility depends on the exact release |
| Deployment (cloud / on-premise / brand-hosted) | Changes interface access and setup |
| Interface access and who grants it | Brand, owner or vendor approval |
| Required workflows and service scope | What we actually connect |
| Brand or owner approval route | Who can say yes, and how long it takes |
| Decision-makers: IT, finance, GM/owner | One conversation with all three |

Until these are confirmed, **do not promise compatibility**, timelines or prices.

## 2. Technical brief for IT (one page)
- **What stays:** your existing PMS remains the system of record for its scope.
- **What Fandaqah adds:** supported back-office, operations, payments and regulatory workflows around it (only approved interfaces).
- **What we need from you:** product/version, deployment, interface documentation or access route, required workflows.
- **How we confirm:** technical meeting, integration assessment, written scope in the proposal.
- **What we never claim:** certification, partnership or endorsement by the PMS vendor.

## 4. Meeting agenda (45 minutes)
1. Current stack and pain points (10)
