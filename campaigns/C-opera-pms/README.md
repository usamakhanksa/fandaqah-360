# Campaign C · Opera and brand-mandated PMS

**Goal:** qualified operational and technology reviews with properties that keep Opera or another mandated PMS, leading to scoped proposals.
**Landing route:** shared URL with `utm_campaign=C_opera_cost_review_2610…` → `/opera-pms/` (see `links.csv`). The campaign id is unchanged so reporting stays continuous.
**Call to action:** Request an operational and technology review · اطلب مراجعة تشغيلية وتقنية

## Message order
Sell the outcome first and the integration second. Every asset follows the same four beats:

| Beat | English | Arabic |
|---|---|---|
| Operational challenge | Running your hotel around Opera through too many systems? | تدير فندقك حول Opera بأنظمة كثيرة؟ |
| Business outcome | One platform for HR, procurement, inventory, finance and compliance. Opera stays your PMS. | منصة واحدة للموارد البشرية والمشتريات والمخزون والمالية والامتثال، ويبقى Opera نظامك. |
| Proof | Fewer systems. Clearer visibility. Room to grow. | أنظمة أقل، ورؤية أوضح، ومساحة للنمو. |
| Review and meeting | Request an operational and technology review. | اطلب مراجعة تشغيلية وتقنية. |

Compatibility, interfaces and approvals still matter. They come after the outcome, as the way we confirm it.

## Buyer
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | GM or owner, IT manager or director, and finance director |
| Wants | Simpler operations and a clearer view across departments, fewer systems to support and controlled technology costs, while keeping the required PMS and brand rules |
| Watch out | A brand name or star rating does not prove the installed PMS |
| Offer | A **scoped proposal**. Never use the A/B package price for Opera integrations, a dedicated environment or customisation |

## Audience and exclusions
- Verified Opera or other mandated-PMS properties only.
- Sales confirms **installed product, version, deployment, interface access and the brand or owner approval route** before promising compatibility.
- Exclude existing customers (A) and properties inside a D group discussion.

## Channels
1. **Small named-account campaign:** an operations overview for the GM or owner, a technical brief for IT and a cost comparison for finance (`sales-brief.md`).
2. **LinkedIn:** connection notes and a four-message sequence per role, plus sponsored content for GM, owner, IT and finance titles at hotels in Saudi Arabia. The live kit is [`linkdein-message.html`](https://usamakhanksa.github.io/fandaqah-360/linkdein-message.html).
3. **Search:** outcome headlines with the integration and cost terms searchers use (`search-ads.csv`).

### Search keywords
`opera back office integration` · `opera pms integration saudi` · `hotel back office software` · `hotel technology cost` ·
`hotel procurement and inventory software` · `hotel hr software saudi` ·
`تكامل الإدارة الخلفية مع أوبرا` · `نظام محاسبة فندقي مع Opera` · `نظام مشتريات ومخزون للفنادق`

**Negatives:** opera browser · opera house · opera tickets · opera music · opera mini · oracle jobs · opera training course · دار الأوبرا · متصفح أوبرا.

> **Trademark note:** "Opera" is an Oracle trademark. Google Ads can restrict trademarks in **ad text** if the owner files a
> complaint, while keywords usually stay allowed. Keep at least three headlines without the name (already in `search-ads.csv`)
> so the ad keeps serving. No third-party logos anywhere; the site carries a trademark notice.

## Sales handoff
The form sends current PMS and version/deployment. Book a **review meeting** first (how the hotel runs today, where work is
repeated, what to consolidate), then the **integration assessment**, then the scoped proposal. Do not advertise "50% savings":
SAR 20,000 vs SAR 10,000 is an illustration only until a like-for-like comparison establishes scope, recurring charges, setup,
support and interfaces.

## Scorecard
Qualified operational and technology reviews · technical meetings · integration assessments · scoped proposals · signed business.
"24/7 local support" and AI claims need evidence (playbook p.11) before use.
