# Campaign C · Opera and brand-mandated PMS

**Goal:** qualified technology cost reviews with properties that must keep Opera or another mandated PMS, leading to scoped proposals.
**Landing route:** shared URL with `utm_campaign=C_opera_cost_review_2610…` → `/opera-pms/` (see `links.csv`).
**Call to action:** Request a technology cost review · اطلب مراجعة تكاليف التقنية

## Buyer
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | IT manager or director and finance director, supported by the GM or owner |
| Wants | Lower overall technology cost and useful connected workflows, while respecting the required PMS and brand rules |
| Watch out | A brand name or star rating does not prove the installed PMS |
| Offer | A **scoped proposal**. Never use the A/B package price for Opera integrations, a dedicated environment or customisation |

## Audience and exclusions
- Verified Opera or other mandated-PMS properties only.
- Sales confirms **installed product, version, deployment, interface access and the brand or owner approval route** before promising compatibility.
- Exclude existing customers (A) and properties inside a D group discussion.

## Channels
1. **Small named-account campaign:** technical brief to IT, cost comparison to finance (`sales-brief.md`).
2. **Search:** relevant terms such as "Opera back office integration" (`search-ads.csv`).
3. **LinkedIn** professional targeting (IT, finance and GM titles at hotels in Saudi Arabia).

### Search keywords
`opera back office integration` · `opera pms integration saudi` · `hotel back office software` · `hotel technology cost` ·
`تكامل الإدارة الخلفية مع أوبرا` · `نظام محاسبة فندقي مع Opera`

**Negatives:** opera browser · opera house · opera tickets · opera music · opera mini · oracle jobs · opera training course · دار الأوبرا · متصفح أوبرا.

> **Trademark note:** "Opera" is an Oracle trademark. Google Ads can restrict trademarks in **ad text** if the owner files a
> complaint, while keywords usually stay allowed. Keep at least three headlines without the name (already in `search-ads.csv`)
> so the ad keeps serving. No third-party logos anywhere; the site carries a trademark notice.

## Sales handoff
The form sends current PMS and version/deployment. Book a **technical meeting**, then an **integration assessment**, then the scoped
proposal. Do not advertise "50% savings": SAR 20,000 vs SAR 10,000 is an illustration only until a like-for-like comparison
establishes scope, recurring charges, setup, support and interfaces.

## Scorecard
Qualified cost reviews · technical meetings · integration assessments · scoped proposals · signed business.
"24/7 local support" and AI claims need evidence (playbook p.11) before use.
