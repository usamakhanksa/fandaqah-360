# Campaign D · Local chains and operators

**Goal:** qualified group discovery workshops with operators responsible for about 10 or more properties, leading to documented opportunities, pilots and signed group agreements.
**Landing route:** shared URL with `utm_campaign=D_group_discovery_2610…` → `/hotel-groups/` (see `links.csv`).
**Call to action:** Explore centralised management for my group · استكشف الإدارة المركزية لمجموعتي

## Message: the portfolio, not the property
Group leaders buy control of the portfolio. Every asset speaks to multi-property management: centralised control, portfolio
visibility, standardised operations, executive reporting, governance, scalability and group-wide decision making. Modules,
feature lists and single-property operations stay out. The final impression is **"a platform that helps me manage multiple
properties more effectively"**, never "hotel software".

## Buyer
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Group owner, CEO or COO, with IT and finance approval; asset managers with ~10+ properties |
| Wants | Centralised control, portfolio-wide visibility and consistent standards as the group grows, with one accountable technology partner |
| DMCs | Relevant only if they actually manage accommodation assets or technology decisions |
| Offer | A discovery workshop, then a **scoped proposal** with setup, ongoing service and agreed customisation priced separately |

## Audience and exclusions
- Named Saudi chain accounts and verified group-level decision-makers.
- Confirm **managed vs owned or franchised** properties, approximate property count and **who controls technology purchasing**.
- Coordinate all contacts under **one account owner**; do not treat every property as an unrelated lead. Existing customers stay in A.

## Channels
1. **Personalised account outreach** from the account owner (`sales-brief.md`).
2. **LinkedIn content** and sponsored posts to group-level titles (CEO, COO, IT director, asset manager); sequence, ads and posts in the [LinkedIn kit](https://usamakhanksa.github.io/fandaqah-360/linkdein-message.html).
3. **Focused invitation** to the discovery workshop, framed as a working session on the group's operating model, not a product demo.

## Sales handoff
The form sends property count and property structure. Book the discovery workshop (agenda in `sales-brief.md`), document the
opportunity, propose a pilot. Demonstrate AI only through approved working use cases.

## Wording rules
"Customised to an agreed scope", never "100% customised". White labelling does **not** imply source-code ownership, unlimited
changes or exclusivity; the contract decides these.
