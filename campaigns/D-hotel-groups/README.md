# Campaign D · Local chains and operators

**Goal:** qualified group discovery meetings with operators responsible for about 10 or more properties, leading to workshops, pilots and signed group agreements.
**Landing route:** shared URL with `utm_campaign=D_group_discovery_2610…` → `/hotel-groups/` (see `links.csv`).
**Call to action:** Book a group discovery meeting · احجز اجتماعاً استكشافياً للمجموعة

## Buyer
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Group owner, CEO or COO, with IT and finance approval; asset managers with ~10+ properties |
| Wants | Group control and a technology partner |
| DMCs | Relevant only if they actually manage accommodation assets or technology decisions |
| Offer | A discovery workshop, then a **scoped proposal** with setup, ongoing service and agreed customisation priced separately |

## Audience and exclusions
- Named Saudi chain accounts and verified group-level decision-makers.
- Confirm **managed vs owned or franchised** properties, approximate property count and **who controls technology purchasing**.
- Coordinate all contacts under **one account owner**; do not treat every property as an unrelated lead. Existing customers stay in A.

## Channels
1. **Personalised account outreach** from the account owner (`sales-brief.md`).
2. **LinkedIn content** and sponsored posts to group-level titles (CEO, COO, IT director, asset manager).
3. **Focused group-demo invitation** to the discovery workshop.

## Assets in this folder
`ad-copy.md` · `reel-storyboard.md` · `sales-brief.md` · `creatives/` (Arabic and English) · `links.csv`
Any sample white-label screen is marked **"Illustrative branding"** (already on every D creative and on the page).

## Sales handoff
The form sends property count and property structure. Book the discovery workshop (agenda in `sales-brief.md`), document the
opportunity, propose a pilot. Demonstrate AI only through approved working use cases.

## Wording rules
"Customised to an agreed scope", never "100% customised". White labelling does **not** imply source-code ownership, unlimited
changes or exclusivity; the contract decides these.

## Scorecard
Qualified group meetings · workshops · documented opportunities · pilots · signed group agreements.
