Questions about Support & Training

Do we need IT staff to use Fandaqah?+

The platform is designed for everyday use, with training resources available. Discuss your existing setup and support requirements during onboarding.

What support comes with each package?+

Basic includes standard support and basic training, Compliance includes priority support and full training, and Fandaqah 360 adds premium support and priority onboarding.

Is Support & Training included in every package?+

Yes. Support & Training is part of the Basic Subscription, the Compliance Package and Fandaqah 360.
