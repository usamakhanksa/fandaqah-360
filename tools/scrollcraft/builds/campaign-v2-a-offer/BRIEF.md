# Campaign A · offer-led content revision (EN/AR) + reel close

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner forwarded two reviews and asked to "enhance content
above comments ... update reels content ... design okay" for `existing-customers/index.html`, acting "like a senior
expert content writer". A revision of the campaign-v2-A build: same grammar, devices and peak, so no new fingerprint
row. Two sections are added (comparison, guarantee) inside the existing design vocabulary.

## The reviews, condensed

- Design 8/10, offer visibility 4/10, use of selling points 3/10: "the page sells an upgrade review process, not the offer".
- Missing in the first seconds: the price, that it will not be repeated, the two-year lock, the 30-day guarantee with
  rollback to the previous plan.
- Also: modules described as features, not outcomes; no before/after comparison; form too long for a known customer;
  FAQ misses the real objections (cost, going back, data, training, will the price return).

## Offer facts (supplied by the owner in the reviews)

- Fandaqah 360, all 20 modules: **SAR 3,495 a year, excluding VAT** (SAR 4,019.25 with 15% VAT; the figure already
  published on the feature pages and landing v2).
- **One-time launch price.** It will not be offered again; standard pricing applies after the launch period.
- **Two-year price lock** with upfront payment: SAR 3,495 × 2 = SAR 6,990 excl. VAT (SAR 8,038.50 with VAT).
- **The Golden Guarantee:** if the upgrade is not right for the property in the first 30 days, a full refund and the
  subscription returned to its previous plan.

## Decisions

1. **The offer is the hero.** Headline: upgrade to Fandaqah 360 and lock in the launch price for two years; the price,
   "excl. VAT" and "one-time launch price" sit under it, before any explanation.
2. **One action label everywhere:** "Lock in my launch price" / «ثبّت سعر الإطلاق», shared with landing v2 and reel B.
3. **Outcomes, not modules:** each module card leads with what changes in the hotel; the module name stays as label.
4. **Before and after:** "A typical plan today" against "After upgrading to Fandaqah 360".
5. **Risk removed in its own section:** the Golden Guarantee, with the seal used on landing v2.
6. **The peak (pinned offer band) does the arithmetic:** per year, two years upfront, with and without VAT.
7. **Form cut to what Fandaqah does not already know:** hotel, name, email or phone, term; modules optional.
8. **FAQ rewritten around objections.** Honest scarcity only: no countdown, no invented standard price, no savings figure.
