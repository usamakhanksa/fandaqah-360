"enhance content and complete properly ... new, stylish, corporate and realistic ... something unique ... show me your

decides structure, depth, the peak and the signature.

## The eight topics (authored)

1. **Vibe:** a group boardroom at golden hour. Composed, exact, quietly proud. References: an annual-report cover,
   an architect's site model, a brand guidelines book.
2. **Journey:** see the portfolio from above; agree what the group shares; watch one view open into each property;
   make the platform wear your name; know what the workshop covers; see the path and how it is priced; settle the
   questions; ask for the meeting.
3. **Energy:** a wide, still hero; a quiet ledger; the one held, moving moment (the platform); a playful studio; calm
   order for the rest; a decisive close.
4. **Feeling and peak:** below.
5. **What no site does:** the platform takes on the visitor's group name and colour, and keeps wearing it, from the
   header to the welcome screen of the close.
6. **Range:** corporate-editorial on the campaign palette, with one colour the visitor chooses.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes, one identity running through them.
8. **Assets:** one owned aerial image; every device, document and screen in markup.

## Grammar

**Co-branded dossier (new).** The page is the visitor's own platform proposal: every chapter carries one identity,
the default "Your hotel group" until the visitor sets theirs, and the setting persists from the header to the close.
Navigation: the site header keeps four anchors, and carries an **identity chip** (mark and group name in the chosen
colour) that opens the studio. Ending: the visitor's branded portal welcome screen beside a two-step request.
Bans: no section counters, no tray, no comparison card, no peach triptych; one pin only.

Why not the others: comp-led conversion (A, C) is taken and the owner wants something new; hotel day and live
surface are taken; filmic one-shot and worldflight would bury dense commercial content; a pure gallery cannot carry
the dashboard.

## Layer contract (hero)

far: aerial portfolio photograph, its own coral thread joining the three properties; mid: three property pins placed
on the buildings, each with a dashed coral drop line; glass: the group board, wearing the visitor's identity,
overlapping the photo's lower edge (occlusion); near: blurred leaf at the trail edge. Type sits on the cream ground,
never on the photograph. Pointer and scroll move each plane at its own rate; still under reduced motion.
